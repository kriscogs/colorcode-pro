Common questions

What happens after five months?

It becomes $159 a month automatically. You'll see the date in the app the whole time, and you can cancel before it if it isn't earning its keep.

What if I cancel?

You keep access to the end of the month you've paid for, and you can export everything — jobs, clients, estimates, payments, photos — in one click. Your data is never held hostage.

Does it cost more for more people?

No. Put your whole crew on it. The price is per shop.

Do my customers need an account?

No. A private customer taps a link and sees their vehicle. Dealerships get a real login because they have several cars and several people.

Can I move my existing jobs in?

Email me. If you can export from what you're using now, I'll do it for you.
